Normal Body, Not Nobody

Looking posh is for people who feel like nobodies and want to feel like somebodies.

They look at normal people and think they are nothing. So they wear something that makes them look posh, and for a moment they feel like somebody.

Now ask yourself: if they cannot afford the posh thing, how do they feel? Like a normal body, and a normal body feels like a nobody. They have split people into two boxes, and the normal body never gets a place in the picture.

The normal body is who you want to be. When it achieves something, it sees that as a gift from Allah and celebrates His kindness with شُكر (shukr, gratitude). The nobody tends toward جحود (juhud, ingratitude), كسل (kasal, laziness), or عجز (ajz, helplessness). The somebody tends toward كِبر (kibr, arrogance) or رياء (riya, showing off).

If buying leather shoes makes you happy and stylish, that is fine. The Prophet ﷺ was asked about exactly this. In Sahih Muslim, Abdullah ibn Mas’ud reported that the Prophet ﷺ said: “He who has in his heart an atom’s weight of pride will not enter Paradise.” A man said: “A man loves his shoes to be nice and his clothes to be nice. Is that pride?” He said: “Allah is beautiful and loves beauty. Pride is rejecting the truth and looking down on people.”

But if you wear leather shoes to make a point that you are better than others, that is a problem.

Next time someone says, “This is a very posh thing, only posh people wear this,” think twice. Is it a posh brand? Do you want to be part of that? Can you wear something that makes you feel good, even if it costs more, without it labelling you as someone who looks down on nobodies? Stay a normal body, but a stylish one.

There is also a hadith on this: “Whoever wears a garment of fame in this world, Allah will dress him in a garment of humiliation on the Day of Resurrection, then set it on fire.” (Abu Dawud, Ibn Majah)

You do not need to dress strangely to fall into this. A small logo that says “I pay ten times more than you” can do more damage to your الآخرة (akhirah, the Hereafter) than any loud outfit. Dress well, enjoy it, thank Allah for it, and do not let your clothes rank anyone.
